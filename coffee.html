The Roast

Small batches,
real origins.

01 — Where It's From

Four origins,
one roaster.

Every bag is roasted and packed on site — Malawi, Rwanda, Colombia, Brazil. 100% Arabica, medium roast, worked in batches small enough that someone actually tastes each one before it goes in a bag.

Mixed nuts on the nose. Caramel and chocolate through the cup. Full body, a clean, crisp acidity behind it — the kind of profile that holds up whether it's pulled as an espresso or poured over.

02 — How It's Made

Espresso, pour-over,
and everything after.

Behind the counter it's a Sanremo machine and a hand pour when the bean calls for it. Nothing rushed, nothing performative — just someone who knows the beans making your coffee.

The Process

A slow pour, a paper filter, a weighed dose — filter coffee done the way it's meant to be, one cup at a time.
