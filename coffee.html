03 / At Home

Brew it well
in your own kitchen.

Aeropress

A medium-fine grind, roughly 15 g of coffee to 250 g of water. Stir, steep briefly, then press slowly and steadily.

Pour-over

A medium grind and about one part coffee to sixteen of water. Wet the grounds and let them bloom for thirty seconds, then pour in slow circles.

Keeping beans

Store them airtight, away from light and heat, and grind just before you brew. Fresh grounds taste noticeably better than a week-old scoop.

At the counter

Dose, bloom, pour. The same steps at home as behind the bar.
