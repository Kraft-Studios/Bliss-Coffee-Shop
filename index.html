Bliss Coffee Roastery

Roasted in small batches, a few streets from where it's poured.

A roastery first. A coffee shop because good coffee deserves a good place to drink it.

01 — The Roast

Small batches,
real origins.

Every bag is roasted and packed on site — Malawi, Rwanda, Colombia, Brazil. 100% Arabica, medium roast, worked in batches small enough that someone actually tastes each one.

Mixed nuts on the nose. Caramel and chocolate through the cup. Full body, a clean, crisp acidity behind it.

Before you scroll any further

Why did the coffee file a police report?
It got mugged.

02 — In The Cup

Espresso, pour-over,
and everything after.

Behind the counter it's a Sanremo machine and a hand pour when the bean calls for it. Nothing rushed, nothing performative — just someone who knows the beans making your coffee.

On The Menu

Hot or iced, espresso-based or filter — the iced mocha is the one people keep coming back for.

03 — The Space

Somewhere to
stay a while.

A garden patio out back, a quiet corner inside if the weather doesn't hold. Coffee, light meals, cakes and something sweet with your cup.

Cakes, snacks and something baked most mornings — the kind of food that's happy to share a table with a flat white.
